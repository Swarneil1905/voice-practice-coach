BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

What feedback will I receive?

Text practice checks coverage against the scenario's rubric and suggests a follow up question. Optional AI coaching adds feedback. Coverage is a practice aid, not an assessment of your ability or interview readiness.

Do I need to turn on my microphone?

No. Text practice works without a microphone. Voice starts only after you consent and choose to begin. Audio is processed by LiveKit and AI providers; this application does not save recordings.

Where are my reviews saved?

Only reviews you choose to save are kept in this browser, up to 20 entries. They do not sync across devices. Export a review if you want a separate copy.

Is voice always available?

Voice and AI coaching share a limited public allowance. When that allowance is reached, those features pause. Text practice remains available, subject to request limits.
